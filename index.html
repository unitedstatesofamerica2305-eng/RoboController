What it does

Small app. Real hardware.

Designed around the essentials: connect, steer, adjust the speed, and stop. No account, no clutter—just a controller for a home-built robot.

01 / CONNECT

Pair and connect

Pair the HC-05 module in Android settings, then connect from inside the app.

02 / DRIVE

Control movement

Send forward, backward, left, right, and stop commands from the controller screen.

03 / TUNE

Adjust motor speed

Use the speed slider to change the motor speed value sent to the Arduino.

Get the app
